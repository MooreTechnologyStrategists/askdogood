Shopping list

Shop your pantry first. Choose frozen produce where it saves money, compare unit prices, and rinse canned beans if desired.

Prep once, eat twice

Cook a batch of quinoa or rice and roast vegetables. Refrigerate leftovers within two hours in shallow containers; use refrigerated leftovers within 3–4 days or freeze. Cook fish to 145°F (63°C). Swap ingredients according to allergies: sesame in hummus/tahini, soy, fish, eggs, dairy, wheat and nuts may be present.
