# MERCH AI DESIGN BRIEFS - JULY 2026

Goal: produce 8 high-conversion, print-ready concept mockups in the next 24 hours.
Primary markets: DMV and the Carolinas.
Business objective: first online sale in 7 days.

## Brand Direction

### AskDoGood
- Tone: hopeful, direct, premium, movement-centered.
- Audience: women and families focused on healing, purpose, and community.
- Visual style: minimal typography, editorial spacing, soft neutral base with bold accent options.
- Brand promise: practical healing, structure, community.

### The Dope Cloud Teacher (DCT)
- Tone: precise, confident, future-facing.
- Audience: cloud/AI learners, tech professionals, students, conference attendees.
- Visual style: clean geometric typography, high-contrast minimal palettes, tech-lux finish.
- Brand promise: build real skills, secure your future, lead with purpose.

## Production Specs
- Formats: t-shirt front, t-shirt back, hoodie front, hoodie back.
- Print area: 12x16 in max; export transparent PNG and SVG if possible.
- Colorways per concept: black garment + cream garment.
- Deliverable per concept:
  - 1 flat design
  - 1 on-model mockup
  - 1 street-style lifestyle mockup
- Typography: avoid script fonts; prioritize legibility from 8-10 feet.
- Constraint: avoid clutter; no more than 2 text blocks per side.

Use this prompt template per concept:

"Create a premium streetwear t-shirt design for [BRAND]. Use the phrase '[FRONT COPY]' on the front and '[BACK COPY]' on the back. Style: minimal, modern typography, high contrast, clean spacing, editorial layout, premium merch aesthetic. Generate black and cream garment variants. Include one flat lay mockup and one lifestyle mockup. Avoid cartoon style and avoid clutter."
