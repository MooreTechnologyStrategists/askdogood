# AskDoGood offering standard
Adopted November 2, 2026.

The mission, vision, DERS pillars and CHIP-aligned program descriptions are maintained in client/src/content/wellnessFramework.ts. Reuse them in approved flyers, partner proposals and onboarding materials.

The signature offer is the Everyday Wellness Plan: a $25 per-person guided group workshop pilot, approximately 75 minutes. Do not accept payments for unscheduled sessions. Confirm date, format, accommodations, materials, cancellation terms and capacity before opening registration. Group quotes are separate. Existing Food With Purpose and Proactive Days self-paced toolkits are separate products and retain their existing checkout and delivery.

Before launching a public workshop:
- Confirm facilitator, date, accessible delivery and booking/cancellation process.
- Prepare the seven-day plan worksheet, grocery-planning activity, sample plan, facilitator guide and task-based assessment.
- Pilot the complete session: brief baseline, demonstration, guided practice, feedback, revision and independent adaptation to a new scenario.
- Test whether participants can build a workable plan and fallback step; record barriers and improve materials.
- Check economics at realistic attendance: preparation, delivery, support, materials and transaction costs.
- Verify the booking, payment, confirmation and follow-up journey end to end.
- Review qualified-provider boundaries and any factual health claims.

DERS is a practical habit framework, not a clinical definition or assessment of health. Spiritual practice is inclusive of faith, purpose and reflection. CHIP alignment is not an endorsement or signed partnership. Specialized maternal/infant content requires appropriately qualified partners.

AskDoGood customer navigation: Eat Well, Spend Less, Find Your Next Step.
Primary guided-offer action: Build my everyday wellness plan.
Partner action: Bring AskDoGood to your community.
Preserve official logos, cream/green/terracotta identity and authentic photography.

Audit the legacy catalog separately before changing product prices, checkout fulfillment or health-related claims. Do not represent the audit as complete.
