# AskDoGood Product Vision

## Positioning

AskDoGood is a holistic life-improvement and community wellness platform built from lived experience.

It helps people move forward through:
- Honest stories
- Practical guidance
- Science-backed wellness education
- Spiritual grounding
- Family and caregiving support
- Career and digital opportunity pathways

## Core Promise

Feel better. Live better. Move forward.

Users should leave with at least one practical action and a stronger sense of hope, clarity, and support.

## Audience Outcomes

Visitors should feel:
- Less alone
- Better informed
- More hopeful
- Ready for one healthy next step
- Ready to join, subscribe, purchase, or share

## Content Pillars

1. Wellness
2. Mind and Spirit
3. Family and Caregiving
4. Life and Opportunity
5. Policy and People (human-impact framing)

## Product Principles

1. Helpful before promotional.
2. Practical before inspirational.
3. Compassion without judgment.
4. Evidence-aware and transparent about what is opinion vs evidence.
5. Personal voice without turning every page into biography.

## Trust Standards

Every evidence-based article should include:
- Author identity
- Reviewed/updated date
- Source links
- Disclaimers (medical, affiliate/sponsored where relevant)

## Experience Direction

AskDoGood should feel like a calm, low-light, welcoming digital sanctuary.

Design language target:
- Warm, grounded, refined
- Culturally respectful influences (Japanese balance, African warmth/pattern, American accessibility/community)
- Clear hierarchy and breathing room
- Gentle motion, not noisy animation

## Business Outcome

Build a trusted platform where users naturally progress from:
- Content -> resource -> subscription -> service/product/community participation

without feeling pushed through a hard-sell funnel.
